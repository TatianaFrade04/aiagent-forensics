## Circumstances of Death

The body was found by fishermen in the early hours of the morning, partially submerged on the bank of a river. The victim was wearing wet clothing and drag marks were visible on the muddy ground. Authorities were alerted immediately. The family had reported the young man missing the previous night after a party at a nearby location.

- Labial and nail cyanosis
- Ecchymosis in the occipital region (possible traumatism from a fall or impact)
- No incised or contused wounds
- Bilateral conjunctival petechial hemorrhages
- Pink frothy discharge from the nostrils and mouth

## Complementary Analyses

- **Diatom analysis:** Species identified are consistent with the local aquatic environment
- **Histology:** Diffuse alveolar edema; fluid with sediment particles present in the alveoli
- **Water analysis:** Comparison between diatoms found in the lungs and river water samples — concordant result

---

## Conclusion

**Cause of death:** Drowning (submersion in aquatic medium).  
**Manner of death:** Accident, with contribution from acute alcohol intoxication.

---

## Remarks

The presence of ethanol at a relevant concentration is consistent with behavioral disinhibition and reduced responsiveness to danger. The occipital ecchymosis is consistent with an accidental fall on the riverbank prior to submersion. No evidence of homicide or third-party involvement was found. The case was closed as an accidental death.
